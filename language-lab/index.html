How the shelf grows

One video can become many rounds.

When a learning pattern stays useful, we do not need a new theory. We can add another session with a new story, skill, or support level.

  1. Watch and traceFind the original case or source behind the video.
  2. Separate evidenceKeep personal reports, explanations, research, and design choices apart.
  3. Build one playable loopMake the idea small enough to do today with sound, sight, writing, and speech.
  4. Replay or add a SessionKeep the method card; grow the library underneath it.
