Ü pronunciation guide: Ü is not a separate letter in the Spanish alphabet. The two dots show that the u is pronounced in words such as pingüino.

Latin American baseline

Spanish spelling is highly regular. Start with five steady vowels, combine them into syllables, then learn the small set of letters that change with context. Tap an example—or reach it with the arrow keys—to hear a Mexican Spanish 2.0 voice.

Five steady vowels

The vowel sound stays clear and short. Unlike English, one written vowel does not keep changing its identity.

Build clean syllables

Special sounds & stress

A few contrasts carry meaning. Regional accents may vary, but the spelling rules remain useful everywhere.

Default stress: words ending in a vowel, n, or s stress the next-to-last syllable. Other endings stress the last. A written accent overrides the default.
